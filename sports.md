### Ultimate
At the beginning of my undergrad I discovered the sport of Ultimate, a dynamic team sport played with a flying disc
(more commonly known as a Frisbee). Being a non-contact sport, it maximising the technical and tactical aspects
and allows for play with mixed-gendered teams. Over the years I have participated in many [competitions](/tournaments.html),
for a number of different clubs and teams.

have coached several mixed and open teams across all levels.

as well as outreach events for organisations including
